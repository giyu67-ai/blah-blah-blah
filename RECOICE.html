Granny Seikos Crab Nabe (Japanese Crab Hot Pot) from Episode 4 of DanDaDan. It is a comforting, umami-rich winter dish featuring fresh snow crab legs and assorted vegetables simmered in a seasoned dashi broth.

this legendary hot pot is a stape for cozy winter nights. the naturally sweet snow crab legs blend perfectly into the savory broth, infusing every single vegetable with a deep ,rich flavor that will warm you right up.


Ingredients List

  • 2 clusters frozen snow crab legs and claws
  • 1 piece dried konbu
  • 4 cups water
  • 1and a half cabbage (cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1 large leek (sliced diagonally)
  • 1 block medium or silken tofu (cut into cubes)
  • 1 pack Enoki or Shiitake mushrooms(stems trimmed)
  • 1 to 2 carrot(sliced thin, optionally cut into flower shapes)
  • 4 tbsp shirodashi sause (for seasoning the broth)
  • 1 tbsp Mirin (red chilli)
  • 1 tbsp Sake
  • sesame sause (for dipping)
  • 1 diced Onion

Step-by-Step Cooking Recipe

1.Prepare the Stock
Place the water and dried konbu in a pot .Let it sock for 20 minutes,then bring it to a gentel simmer.Remove the konbu just before boiling .

2.Season the Base
Whisk the shirodashi, mirin,and sake. into the hot stock.

3.Build-up the vegetable Base
Arrange heavy vegetable first-layer cabbage at the bottom ,then place leeks,tofu blocks,carrot, and mushrooms neatly around thepot.

4.Add the Crab legs and claws directly on top of the vegetable.
5. Simmer and cook
Cover the pot with a lid. Bring everything to a boil over medium-high heat, then lower the flame andSimmer for8 to 10 minutes.
